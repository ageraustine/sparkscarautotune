Company Overview

Your trusted automotive partner in Nairobi

SparksCar Auto Tune Ltd is a leading automotive service provider, specializing in the repair, servicing and maintenance of vehicles across all makes and models. Based in Kahawa Wendani, Nairobi — with two branches, HQ Bay and Bay 2 — we have earned a reputation for reliability, precision and excellent customer service.

Whether it's routine maintenance, major mechanical repairs or preventative services for a privately owned vehicle, or a full accident assessment and repair for an insurance-referred vehicle, our experienced team is committed to ensuring every vehicle we work on receives the best possible care — with a focus on quality, safety and performance.
