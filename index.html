Company Overview

Your trusted automotive partner in Nairobi

SparksCar Auto Tune Ltd is a leading automotive service provider, specializing in the repair, servicing and maintenance of vehicles across all makes and models. Based in Kahawa Wendani, Nairobi — with our HQ Bay and Bay 2 branches — we've earned a reputation for reliability, precision and excellent customer service.

Whether it's a privately owned vehicle needing routine maintenance, or an insurance-referred vehicle needing a full accident assessment and repair, our experienced team handles every job with a focus on quality, safety and performance.
