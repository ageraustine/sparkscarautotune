Company Overview

Your trusted automotive partner in Nairobi

SparksCar Auto Tune Ltd is a leading automotive service provider, specializing in the repair, servicing and maintenance of vehicles across all makes and models. Based in Kahawa Wendani, Nairobi, we have earned a reputation for reliability, precision and excellent customer service.
